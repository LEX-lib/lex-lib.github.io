<objective>
Complete the roster's lifecycle — edit, deactivate/reactivate, delete — and then prove the two
server-side assertions this phase exists to establish: an unauthenticated request cannot read
the roster (VERIFY-03), and the write rules are admin-gated rather than merely
authenticated-gated (ROSTER-06's server half).

Purpose: everything before this plan is client-side convenience. The enforceable boundary in
Phase 38 is the PocketBase rule text, and its two failure modes are silent — an empty-string
`listRule` reads like "unconfigured" while meaning *public*, and a write rule written as "is
logged in" instead of "is admin" hands every boarder full roster control with no error anywhere.
Neither surfaces without a deliberate probe against the live instance, and a probe against an
empty roster would pass vacuously.

Output: the full row-action cluster, a seeded roster verified in-app end to end, and both live
probes with their transcripts recorded in `38-COLLECTION.md`.
</objective>

## Flagged planner assumption (unresolved edge-probe row)

- **ROSTER-04 (unclassified) — assumption:** "mark a boarder inactive, keeping payment history
  intact" is assumed to mean a reversible two-state flag on the boarder row and nothing more.
  Deactivation is assumed to have **no** cascading effect in Phase 38: it does not hide the
  boarder from the roster (D-38-16 keeps one list), does not touch any payment record, does not
  clear the account link, and does not prevent editing. The "keeping payment history intact"
  half of the requirement is vacuous in this phase by construction — `paytime_payments.boarder`
  does not exist until Phase 39, so no payment can reference a boarder and there is no history
  for deactivation to threaten. The half that *is* exercised here is the flag's reversibility
  and its single write path. Filtering inactive boarders out of the payment selector is
  BEHALF-03 in Phase 40; refusing to delete a boarder with history is ROSTER-07 in Phase 39.

## Deferred to Phase 39, deliberately, not gaps

- **ROSTER-07 (refuse delete with history)** — Phase 39. Phase 39 adds the has-history refusal
  **on top of the Delete button this plan introduces**, rather than introducing the button
  (D-38-15's recorded consequence #2).
- **The non-admin-token rule sweep** — Phase 39's VERIFY-02, which the ROADMAP already scopes as
  "both an admin token and a non-admin token ... all five rules exercised together in one pass".
  Recorded above as a `backstop` truth so verification abstains rather than passing silently.
- **The delete guard gap** — CONTEXT.md D-38-15 records this as a considered user override, not
  an oversight: in Phase 38 the admin-only `deleteRule` is the only guard standing behind the
  Delete button, and that was accepted on the grounds that a boarder mistyped five minutes ago
  should be removable rather than permanently deactivated. Do not re-litigate it; do not soften
  the button.
