## The two outcomes Probe 9 must tolerate

`39-RESEARCH.md` § 6 surfaced a LOW-confidence, unofficial report (GitHub issue #6498) that PocketBase
may **refuse** to delete a record referenced elsewhere by a `required: true` plus
`cascadeDelete: false` relation, rather than succeeding and leaving an orphan. That is exactly the
config `paytime_payments.boarder` now carries. Official docs are silent either way.

| Branch | Observation | VERIFY-05 verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **ORPHANED** | The boarder delete returns 2xx; the payment row survives with a `boarder` value pointing at a now-nonexistent id | **PASS** — matches D-39-13/D-39-16's stated expectation |
| **REFUSED** | The boarder delete returns a 4xx naming the required relation; both the boarder and the payment survive untouched | **PASS** — the payment history survives by an even stronger mechanism than expected |

Do **not** write an acceptance criterion that only passes on one branch, and do not treat a 4xx delete
response as a test failure. Both satisfy VERIFY-05's real goal: no payment history is destroyed.
Record which one actually fired.
