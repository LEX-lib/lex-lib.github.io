## Zod-layer decision (Claude's discretion, D-39-CONTEXT)

`boarder` and `recorded_by` stay **out of `paymentSchema`**, exactly as `user` is today. They are
identity fields supplied by the component from `useBoarderRoster`/`authStore`, never typed by a
user, so validating them in Zod would only force the component to inject them before `parse()`
for no gain. The compile-time guarantee comes from `AddPaytimePayment` requiring both fields, and
the runtime guarantee from `ManagePayment`'s null-boarder guard. **Consequence, stated plainly:**
PocketBase v0.23+ has no per-field defaults, so there is no server-side backstop for either field
beyond PocketBase's own `required` validation once 39-04 flips `boarder` to required — any future
write path that bypasses `mapToCreatePayment` loses the guarantee. `paymentSchema.ts` and
`paymentSchema.spec.ts` are therefore **not modified in this phase**.

**Detector fired** (`detected: true`; signals `pluralization` — "fallback", "also", "another" — and
`optional`). Not a false positive: this phase moves the payment's identity anchor.

- **Noun that is now primary:** the **boarder**. `paytime_boarders` is the canonical payment
  subject. The **account** (`users`) is demoted to a *detail of one variant* — a boarder that
  happens to have a linked account. The majority variant is the accountless boarder (6 of 7 roster
  rows carry `user: ""` per `38-COLLECTION.md`), so the account-bearing case is the special case,
  not the default.
- **Decision: `promote`.** D-39-01 renames `user` -> `recorded_by` rather than adding a third
  relation, and that **is a promote, not an add-alongside** — stated explicitly here rather than
  left implied. The account relation is not kept as a co-equal subject; it survives only in a
  demoted role (who typed the row), and the old subject name is retired outright.
- **Invariant test encoding the generalized intent (authored as a real task, 39-03 Task 3):** an
  accountless boarder's payment round-trips through the same `mapToCreatePayment` write path and
  the same `MonthlyReport` grouping read path as a linked boarder's. It goes red the instant a
  future phase reintroduces the account-as-subject assumption (e.g. by making `recorded_by`
  load-bearing for grouping, or by deriving the subject from `expand.recorded_by`).
</assumption_delta_decision>
